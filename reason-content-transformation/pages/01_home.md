## 7 · FAQ
**H2:** Questions students ask first
- **How much does counselling cost?** Your first session is free. After that you get a written fee schedule — also public on our Fees page — before you agree to anything.
- **Which countries do you cover?** USA, Canada, UK, Australia, New Zealand, Japan and European countries such as Germany. Each guide shows current requirements and official links.
- **Can you guarantee my visa or admission?** No. Only the institution and the government decide. We make sure your file is complete, consistent and on time.
- **How long does a visa take?** It depends on the country and season. We give you the current official time at your first session and link the source.
- **How much are IELTS and PTE classes?** IELTS: Rs. 8,000 for 6 weeks. PTE: Rs. 10,000 for 4 weeks. These are class fees; the exam fee is paid separately to the test provider.
