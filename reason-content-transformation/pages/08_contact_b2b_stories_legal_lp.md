# C · Student stories  ·  `/stories` *(hidden until real)*

**Publish a story only when all are true:** (1) signed consent from the student (and parent if under 18 – then don't publish their face or full name), (2) the outcome is documented (offer letter, CAS, permit, or a public review link), (3) the student has seen the final wording.

**Card format:** Photo · First name + initial · Destination · Intake · Course & institution · Outcome (one line) · 1–2 sentence quote · "See proof" link.
**Never:** invented quotes, "success rate", ranking by outcome.

**Page copy (when ≥ 3 stories exist):**
- H1: **Student stories**
- Intro: *Real students, shared with their written consent.*
- Footer line: *Every result depends on the student, the institution and the government's decision. We can't promise yours.*

**Consent line for the form:** *I agree that Reasons Education may publish my name, photo and the details above on its website and social channels. I can withdraw this by writing to info@reasons.edu.np.*
