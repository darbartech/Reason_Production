## Quick answer
Apply for a UK Student visa after you hold a **Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS)**. **You must show tuition plus living costs held for 28 days. For applications from 30 Nov 2026 that is £1,570 per month in London and £1,203 elsewhere, for up to 9 months.**

## Who it suits
- Students who want a shorter, focused degree
- Students who can show funds held for 28 days

**Think twice if:** you rely on post-study work to repay costs — rules are changing and the outcome is uncertain.

## Requirements (typical)
1. Offer from a UKVI-licensed sponsor and a **CAS**
2. English test (university score; UKVI-approved test where required)
3. Proof of funds: tuition + maintenance, held 28 days
4. Passport, transcripts, SOP/personal statement
5. **TB test** from an approved clinic (Nepal)
6. Immigration Health Surcharge (paid in the application)
7. Identity check with the UKVI ID Check app and biometrics

## Visa steps
1. Choose a course at a UKVI-licensed institution
2. Take your English test
3. Apply to the university and receive an offer
4. Meet conditions, pay the deposit, and request your CAS
5. Apply for the Student visa online and pay IHS
6. Verify identity and give biometrics
7. Decision. Your immigration status is held online (eVisa), not as a BRP card

## Money & common mistakes
**Plan around the 30 Nov 2026 date.** The funds figure depends on the date you apply, not your CAS date. Ask us which applies to you.
**Common refusal reasons**
- Funds not held for the full 28 days, or documents in the wrong format
- Study plan or progression that doesn't make sense
- Missing TB certificate, or from an unapproved clinic

## FAQ
**What is a CAS?** A reference number your university issues once you meet its conditions. You need it to apply for the Student visa.
**How long can I stay after graduating?** Currently 2 years on the Graduate route (3 for PhD). For applications from 1 Jan 2027 it is 18 months for bachelor's and master's graduates, though the government has announced 2 years for graduates of some universities. Check gov.uk.
**Is the BRP still issued?** No. BRPs are being replaced by eVisas. You verify identity with the UKVI ID Check app.
**How much money do I need?** First-year tuition plus £1,570 per month in London or £1,203 elsewhere, up to 9 months, for applications from 30 Nov 2026. Before that date: £1,529 and £1,171.
