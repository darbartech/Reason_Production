## Quick answer
Apply for a **fee-paying student visa** after you hold an **Offer of Place** and paid the fees. You must show **NZ$20,000 per year** for living costs (courses of a year or more). **Since 3 Nov 2025, students can work up to 25 hours a week in term.**

## Who it suits
- Students who prefer smaller cities and campus support
- Students targeting Level 7+ qualifications

**Think twice if:** you plan to rely on work hours to cover costs, or you may switch provider or level (a new visa is often needed).

## Requirements (typical)
1. Offer of Place from an approved provider
2. English test result
3. Proof of tuition payment and living costs
4. Passport, transcripts, SOP
5. Medical and police certificates when requested
6. Biometrics

## Visa steps
1. Choose a course and provider
2. Take your English test
3. Apply and receive an Offer of Place
4. Pay tuition fees (or arrange a deposit as required)
5. Prepare financial documents
6. Apply online; complete medical and biometrics
7. Decision and pre-departure briefing

## Money & common mistakes
- **Switching provider or lowering your study level** usually needs a new student visa.
- **Funds that don't match** the plan in your application.
- **Late applications** near the start date.

## FAQ
**What are the work rights?** Up to 25 hours a week during term (from 3 Nov 2025) and full-time during scheduled breaks for qualifying courses. Existing visa holders may need to apply for a variation.
**Is there a post-study work visa?** Yes. Level 7+ qualifications make it easier to qualify; conditions apply.
**How much is the cost of living?** Immigration New Zealand asks you to show NZ$20,000 per year for courses of a year or more.
