## Quick answer
You apply for an **F-1 visa** after a SEVP-approved school issues your **Form I-20**. You pay the SEVIS fee, complete the DS-160, and attend an interview at the US Embassy in Kathmandu. **Since 15 Sep 2026 *(unless a court has paused it – check)*, F-1 students are admitted until a fixed date matching the programme length (up to 4 years, plus a 30-day grace period) instead of "duration of status".**

## Who it suits
- Students with a clear course and a funded first year
- Students who want research-focused or STEM programmes

**Think twice if:** your funds are thin, your finances can't be shown clearly, or you can't explain how the course fits your plans.

## Requirements (typical)
1. Admission and Form I-20 from a SEVP-approved school
2. English test (and SAT/ACT or GRE/GMAT where the programme asks)
3. SEVIS fee payment and receipt
4. DS-160 confirmation page and appointment
5. Passport valid for the required period
6. Proof of funds and sponsor documents
7. Transcripts and a study plan you can explain

## Visa steps
1. Shortlist SEVP-approved schools
2. Take English and any required tests
3. Apply; receive admission and the I-20
4. Pay the SEVIS fee; complete the DS-160
5. Pay the visa fee and book an appointment (Kathmandu)
6. Attend the interview
7. Decision and pre-departure briefing

## Money & common mistakes
**Know your fixed stay.** Your admit-until date follows your programme length. If you need longer, you apply for an extension with USCIS (Form I-539). Plan your course length realistically.
**Common refusal reasons**
- Not showing strong ties and clear plans (Section 214(b))
- Funds that can't be traced or explained
- Answers at interview that don't match the file

## FAQ
**What is the I-20?** The Form I-20 is issued by a SEVP-approved school. You need it to pay the SEVIS fee and apply for the F-1 visa.
**What changed on 15 Sep 2026?** The "duration of status" rule was replaced with a fixed admission period (programme length, up to 4 years) plus a 30-day grace period. Check Study in the States for the latest, including any court decisions.
**Are scholarships available to Nepali students?** Some universities give merit awards and graduate assistantships. Check each university's page; we help you find ones you can apply for.
**How long is the interview wait?** It varies by season. Check appointment availability on the US travel site and book as early as your I-20 allows.
