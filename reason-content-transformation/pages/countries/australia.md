## Quick answer
You apply for a **Student visa (subclass 500)** after receiving a **Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE)**. You must meet the **Genuine Student** requirement, show **AUD 29,710** for 12 months of living costs plus tuition and travel, and hold health cover (OSHC).

## Who it suits
- Students with a genuine, well-explained study plan
- Students comfortable with a higher cost of living

**Think twice if:** your plan changes course or level without a clear reason, or your funds can't be shown for 12 months.

## Requirements (typical)
1. Offer and **CoE** from a CRICOS-registered provider
2. **Genuine Student** statement
3. English test result
4. Proof of funds (living + tuition + travel)
5. **OSHC** health cover for the visa period
6. Passport, transcripts, SOP
7. Health and character checks when requested

## Visa steps
1. Choose a course at a CRICOS-registered provider
2. Take your English test
3. Apply and receive a Letter of Offer
4. Accept; pay deposit and OSHC
5. Receive the CoE
6. Apply for the subclass 500 online, with GS statement and funds
7. Health checks if requested; decision

## Money & common mistakes
**Genuine Student.** It replaced the old GTE statement for applications from 23 Mar 2024. Explain your reasons for this course, this provider and Australia, in your own words, consistent with your past study and work.
**Common refusal reasons**
- Study plan that doesn't match your background or plans
- Funds that can't be traced or explained
- Inconsistent answers across the application

## FAQ
**What is the Genuine Student requirement?** It is an assessment of whether you are coming to Australia for a genuine course of study. It replaced the GTE statement from 23 Mar 2024.
**Can I stay after graduation?** You may be able to apply for a Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485). Duration and age limits apply; check Home Affairs.
**What is OSHC?** Overseas Student Health Cover is compulsory health insurance for student visa holders.
**Why do processing times differ by university?** The government uses a planning level and priority tiers. Ask us how your shortlist providers are placed.
