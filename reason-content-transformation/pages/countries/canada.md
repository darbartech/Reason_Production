## Quick answer
To apply for a Canadian study permit from Nepal you need an acceptance letter from a Designated Learning Institution (DLI), a Provincial or Territorial Attestation Letter (PAL/TAL) in most cases, and proof of funds. **From 1 Sep 2026, a single applicant outside Québec must show CAD 23,448 for living costs, plus first-year tuition and travel.**

## Who it suits
- Students with a clear course and a funded first year
- Students who accept that work and PR rules change often

**Think twice if:** you can't show the full funds, your course doesn't match your past study, or you rely on part-time work to pay tuition.

## Requirements (typical)
1. Letter of Acceptance from a DLI
2. **PAL/TAL** – most applicants. Master's and PhD students at public institutions are exempt from 1 Jan 2026 *(confirm on IRCC)*
3. English test result (institution's minimum; often IELTS 6.0–6.5 overall)
4. Proof of funds (current IRCC figure + tuition + travel)
5. Passport, transcripts, SOP
6. Police certificate and medical exam, when requested
7. Biometrics (fingerprints and photo)

## Visa steps
1. Shortlist DLI programmes that fit your results and budget
2. Take your English test
3. Apply and receive the Letter of Acceptance
4. Obtain the PAL/TAL (the institution usually requests it)
5. Pay the deposit and gather proof of funds
6. Apply online to IRCC; give biometrics; complete medical if asked
7. Decision, then pre-departure briefing

*The Student Direct Stream (SDS) closed on 8 Nov 2024. Don't use guides that still mention it.*

## Money & common mistakes
**Proof of funds.** Show the amount above plus first-year tuition and travel, in documents IRCC accepts (bank statements, GIC, loan sanction, sponsor documents). If your programme is longer than a year, explain how you'll pay the rest.
**Common refusal reasons**
- Funds not shown clearly or documents don't match
- Study plan that doesn't fit your background or career plan
- Missing or inconsistent documents

## FAQ
**Can I work while studying in Canada?** Yes. Up to 24 hours a week off campus during term, and full-time during scheduled breaks, if you meet IRCC conditions.
**Do I need a PAL?** Most applicants do. Master's and PhD students at public institutions are exempt from 1 Jan 2026. Your institution tells you which applies.
**How much money must I show?** CAD 23,448 for living costs for a single applicant outside Québec (from 1 Sep 2026), plus first-year tuition and travel.
**Is the SDS still available?** No. It closed on 8 Nov 2024.
