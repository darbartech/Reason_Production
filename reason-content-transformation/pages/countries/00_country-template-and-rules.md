# Country page – template and rules

Applies to all `/countries/*` pages. The seven pages that follow fill this template.

## 7 blocks (replaces the old 11)
1. **Header** – H1, one-line overview, *Last checked · Reviewed by*
2. **Snapshot table** – the facts people compare
3. **Who it suits / think twice**
4. **Requirements checklist** (≤ 7)
5. **Visa steps** (≤ 7, numbered)
6. **Money & common mistakes** – proof-of-funds detail + 3 refusal reasons
7. **FAQ (4) + official sources + CTA**

## Snapshot fields (always in this order)
Typical tuition per year · Proof of funds (official, with date) · Main intakes · Work while studying · After you graduate · English tests

## Rules
- **Every figure: value + date + link.** No date → don't publish.
- **"Typical" means a range from 3 named institutions** your students use. Keep the list in your working notes.
- **English scores:** write "typical; each institution sets its own".
- **No scholarships section** unless a named, verified scholarship is open to Nepali students (link to it).
- **No "Why Reasons" section** – one closing line + CTA.
- **Title tag pattern (≤ 60):** `Study in [Country] from Nepal: Costs & Visa (2026)`
- **H1 pattern:** `Study in [Country] from Nepal: costs, intakes and visa steps`
- **Meta pattern (≤ 155):** `[Key fact]. [Intakes]. [English test]. [Visa step]. Free counselling in Kathmandu.`
- **Schema:** `BreadcrumbList` + `Article` (author, `dateModified`) + `FAQPage`.
- **Review cadence:** USA, UK monthly · Canada, Australia, NZ quarterly · Germany, Japan quarterly.
