## Quick answer *(box under H1 – this is what Google/AI quote)*
Most students start **9–12 months before the intake**. The path is: choose a course and country → take an English test → apply → accept an offer → show proof of funds → apply for the visa → prepare to travel. Requirements differ by country and institution, so check each country guide.

## 1 · The six steps
| # | Step | When (typical) | You | We |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Choose** a course and country | 12–9 months before | Share results, budget, goals | Build a ranked shortlist |
| 2 | **Prove English** (IELTS, PTE, TOEFL…) | 10–6 months before | Book and sit the test | Run classes and mock tests |
| 3 | **Apply** to institutions | 9–6 months before | Provide documents, SOP, references | Check each file, submit, follow up |
| 4 | **Accept an offer** | 6–4 months before | Choose, pay deposit if required | Compare offers with you |
| 5 | **Apply for the visa** | 4–2 months before | Show proof of funds | Review forms and evidence; practise interview |
| 6 | **Prepare to travel** | Last 4–6 weeks | Book flights, housing | Pre-departure briefing |

*Timings are typical. Your counsellor sets exact dates from the institution's deadlines.*

## 2 · Documents you'll usually need
- Passport (valid at least 6 months beyond your planned stay)
- Academic transcripts and certificates (SEE, +2, Bachelor's)
- English test result
- Statement of Purpose (SOP) — **written by you**; we review and coach
- Recommendation letters (when the course asks for them)
- Proof of funds (bank statements, loan sanction, sponsor documents)
- Police and medical certificates (when the country requires them)
- Nepal **NOC** before you pay tuition abroad by bank *(check the current process with the Ministry of Education)*

*The exact list depends on the country, course and institution.*
