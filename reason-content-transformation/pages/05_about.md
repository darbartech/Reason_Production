## 1 · Who we are
**H2:** A small team with one process we trust.
Reasons Education is a study abroad consultancy and test-preparation centre at Indreni Complex, New Baneshwor. We started in 2015 and help students choose a course and country, apply, prepare for IELTS/PTE and apply for a visa.
We tell you when a plan doesn't fit your results, budget or timeline, and suggest one that does. We'd rather you study somewhere realistic than chase a rejection.
