## Service blocks *(identical structure; use anchors #counselling #applications #visa #ielts)*

### Study abroad counselling `#counselling`
**You get:** a ranked shortlist of courses and countries that fit your results, budget and timeline.
**You bring:** transcripts, any English test result, budget range.
**Time:** first session about 30 minutes — free.

### Applications & documents `#applications`
**You get:** every document checked, each application submitted and followed up, copies of every email and offer.
**You bring:** course choices, SOP draft, referee details.
**Note:** we review and coach your SOP. The words and experience must be yours.

### Visa support `#visa`
**You get:** the checklist for your country, review of proof-of-funds documents, form checks and interview practice where an interview is required.
**You bring:** offer letter, passport, financial documents.
**Note:** we can't promise a visa. We tell you where your file is strong or weak before you submit.

### IELTS & PTE classes `#ielts`
**You get:** classes taught here, a mock test every Sunday, feedback on each module.
**Fees:** IELTS Rs. 8,000 (6 weeks) · PTE Rs. 10,000 (4 weeks). → [Class details](/ielts)
