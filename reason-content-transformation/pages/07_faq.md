## Planning & money
**When should I start?** About 9–12 months before your intake. Course deadlines differ, and some close early.
**How much money do I need to show?** It depends on the country. As of 2 Nov 2026: Canada CAD 23,448 (single applicant), UK £1,570/month in London from 30 Nov 2026 (£1,529 before), Australia AUD 29,710, New Zealand NZ$20,000, Germany €11,904. Add first-year tuition. See each guide.
**What is an NOC and do I need one?** Nepal requires a No Objection Certificate before you pay tuition abroad through a bank. Ask us about the current process; the Ministry of Education issues it.
**Can I work while I study?** Yes, with limits. Canada 24 hours/week and New Zealand 25 hours/week during term. UK, Australia, US and Japan limits differ. See the country guide.
**What happens after I graduate?** Post-study work rules differ and are changing. For example, the UK Graduate route becomes 18 months for applications from 1 Jan 2027, though the government has announced 2 years for graduates of some universities (details pending).

## Applications & visas
**What is the minimum GPA for Canada?** There is no national minimum. Each institution sets its own, often around 55–60% or 2.8 GPA. We check the course page with you.
**How long does a visa take?** It varies by country and season. We give you the current official processing time and link the source.
**Do you help with the SOP?** We review and coach. The words and experience must be yours. We help with structure, evidence and consistency with the rest of your file.
**What makes visas get refused?** Common reasons: unclear proof of funds, a study plan that doesn't match your background, and missing or inconsistent documents.
**Do I need a Schengen visa to study in Europe?** No. You apply for a national (Type D) student visa and then a residence permit.

## IELTS & PTE
**How much are IELTS/PTE classes?** IELTS: Rs. 8,000 for 6 weeks. PTE: Rs. 10,000 for 4 weeks. The exam fee is separate and paid to the test provider.
**Do you run mock tests?** Yes. Enrolled students sit a full mock test every Sunday and get feedback.
**Can I meet the trainer first?** Yes. Ask to meet your batch trainer before you enrol.
**Which test should I take?** Check the institution's accepted tests and scores first. We'll help you choose.
