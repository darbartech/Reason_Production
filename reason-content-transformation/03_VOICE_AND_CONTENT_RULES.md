# 03 · Voice & Content Rules

One page the whole team (and any AI tool) follows when writing or editing website content.

## 1. Who we write for
- **Primary reader:** a 17–24-year-old Nepali student on a phone, often on mobile data.
- **Second reader:** the **parent** who decides about money. Parents search for *cost, safety, proof, "can I visit the office?"*.
- **Third reader:** Google and AI assistants, which quote pages that answer a question **directly, with a date and a source**.

## 2. Voice – four words
**Plain. Direct. Calm. Honest.**

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Your first session is free." | "We provide unparalleled, world-class guidance." |
| "We can't promise a visa. Only the embassy decides." | "100% success", "guaranteed", "hassle-free" |
| "Canada needs CAD 23,448 in proof of funds (from 1 Sep 2026)." | "Substantial financial evidence is required." |
| "Bring your marksheets and passport." | "Please ensure all requisite documentation is furnished." |

**Banned words** (unprovable or empty): *best, top-rated, leading, world-class, dream, hassle-free, guaranteed, 100%, expert (without a name and credential), seamless, cutting-edge, unlock, journey* (use sparingly), *premier*.

## 3. Writing rules
1. **Answer first.** The first sentence under every heading answers the heading.
2. **One idea per section.** One H2 = one job.
3. **Sentences ≤ 20 words. Paragraphs ≤ 3 lines on mobile.** Target reading age ≈ 13 (Grade 8).
4. **Numbers beat adjectives.** Every number has: *value + currency + "as of" date + link*.
5. **NPR first for Nepali readers** where we can convert (and show the rate date). Otherwise show the local currency and say so.
6. **Lists over paragraphs** for steps, documents, requirements (max 7 items).
7. **One primary CTA per screen.** Same wording everywhere: **"Book a free session"**. Secondary: **"Chat on WhatsApp"**.
8. **Don't repeat.** If a fact lives on another page, link to it. Say it once, where it matters most.
9. **Say what we *can't* do** once, on the pages where people decide (Study abroad, FAQ, Contact).
10. **Use the student's words:** "visa", "IELTS", "SOP", "bank statement", "intake" – no jargon like "stakeholders", "pathways".

## 4. Page skeleton (every content page)
```
H1  (≤ 60 chars, contains the main search phrase)
Intro (≤ 25 words, answers "what is this page for?")
Quick answer / key facts box   ← what Google & AI quote
Body: 3–6 sections, each: H2 + ≤ 80 words + optional list
One CTA block
"Last checked: <date> · Reviewed by <name>" + sources
```

## 5. Claim rules (legal + trust)
| Allowed | Needs proof first | Never |
|---|---|---|
| "Since 2015" | Student counts, success rates, rankings | "Guaranteed visa / admission / scholarship" |
| Service list; fees in writing | "Experienced", "certified" → name + credential | "High acceptance rate" |
| Official facts with source + date | Testimonials → signed consent + proof link | Fake or unnamed reviews |
| "We work with X" **only** with a written agreement | Partner logos | Undisclosed commissions |

## 6. Naming (use exactly)
- **Brand:** Reasons Education (legal: Reasons Education Foundation)
- **Tagline:** Study abroad counselling · IELTS & PTE
- **Terms:** "free session" (not "free consultation/counselling" interchangeably), "IELTS & PTE classes", "visa", "SOP (Statement of Purpose)", "proof of funds"
- **Countries:** USA, Canada, UK, Australia, New Zealand, Germany/Europe, Japan

## 7. Date & figure format
- Dates: **2 Nov 2026** (not 02/11/26).
- Money: **CAD 23,448**, **Rs 8,000**, **£1,570/month**.
- Always "as of <date>" next to a changing figure, and a "Last checked" line per page.

## 8. Bilingual (English + Nepali) – recommended, light version
- Add a short Nepali line to the **Home hero, Contact, and each country page intro** (parents read Nepali first).
- Example: *विदेशमा पढ्ने योजना, एक–एक चरणमा।* · *पहिलो काउन्सेलिङ निःशुल्क।*
- Have a native speaker proofread every Nepali line; keep names of tests/visas in English.

## 9. Accessibility in writing
- Link text says where it goes: "Canada study permit guide", not "click here".
- Buttons start with a verb: "Book a free session", "See Canada requirements".
- Alt text describes the photo's *information*; decorative images get empty alt.
- Don't put essential text only inside images or video.

## 10. Update protocol (stops content going stale)
- Every country page has an **owner** and a **review date**; reviewed **monthly** (USA, UK) or **quarterly** (others) – see calendar in `02_FACT_BASE_2026.md`.
- Change a figure → update the "Last checked" date **and** the sitemap `lastmod`.
- Keep all fees in `pricing.ts`; all contact details in `company.ts`; never type them into page copy.
