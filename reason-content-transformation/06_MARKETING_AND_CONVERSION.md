# 06 · Digital Marketing & Conversion Plan

## 1. Positioning (one sentence)
**"The Kathmandu study abroad consultancy that tells you the truth, shows its fees, and teaches your IELTS/PTE in the same office."**

Why it works in 2026: Nepal's new rules push every consultancy toward licence disclosure and published fees. Most competitors will comply grudgingly; you can make it your **brand**.

## 2. Audience & what each needs to see
| Audience | Worry | Proof to show |
|---|---|---|
| Student (17–24) | "Will I get in? Can I afford it?" | Eligibility quiz, country guides with real numbers, free session |
| Parent | "Is this safe? What will it really cost?" | Licence no., Fees page, office photos, named team, written agreement |
| Returning student (IELTS → counselling) | "Will the same people handle everything?" | One-office promise, counsellor + trainer names |
| Local agent / institution | "Are you reliable?" | Clear B2B page (after legal review), documented process |

## 3. Funnel & the single next step

| Stage | Visitor action | Content | Conversion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discover | Search, Facebook/TikTok/Instagram, Maps | Country guides, short videos, blog | Click to site / GBP |
| Evaluate | Reads a country page, FAQ | Quick answers, dated facts, official links | Eligibility quiz / WhatsApp |
| Decide | Compares consultancies | Licence, Fees, team, reviews, office | **Book a free session** |
| Meet | Free session | Written shortlist | Sign service agreement |
| Retain | IELTS → application → visa | Updates at each stage | Reviews, referrals |

## 4. Offers (keep it to three)
1. **Free first session (30 min)** – primary.
2. **Free eligibility check** (online quiz) – top-of-funnel.
3. **Country requirement sheet (PDF, 2 pages)** – gated by phone/WhatsApp opt-in; one per country; updated with "Last checked" date.

*Avoid discounts and urgency tricks ("only 3 seats"). They conflict with an honesty brand and the 2026 advertising rules.*

## 5. Channels

| Channel | Role | Cadence | Content rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Google Business Profile** | #1 local lead source | Weekly post, photos monthly, reply to every review | NAP identical to site; link to /contact |
| **Facebook (page + groups)** | Main platform for Nepali parents | 3 posts/week | Country updates, office photos, FAQs, event notices |
| **Instagram / TikTok / Reels** | Students | 3 short videos/week | One fact per video; on-screen date; link in bio |
| **YouTube** | Evergreen explainers | 2/month | "Canada proof of funds 2026", "UK funds from 30 Nov" |
| **WhatsApp Business** | Conversion | Daily | Greeting message, quick replies, labels (new/shortlist/documents/visa), catalog of services |
| **Email** | Light | Monthly | "What changed this month" digest |
| **Paid (Meta/Google)** | Test after site is ready | Small budget, 4-week tests | Send traffic to country pages / landing pages, not Home |

**Rules for ads:** include licence number; no "guaranteed", no "100%", no fake urgency; show the free-session offer; always state "We can't guarantee a visa or offer".

## 6. Social proof protocol
- Collect consent in writing (see consent line in `pages/08_…`).
- Prefer **documents** (offer letter, CAS, permit) over adjectives; blur personal data.
- Ask for **Google reviews** from every student at defined moments (after first session, after offer, after visa) – never pay, gate or script reviews.
- Use *numbers* only if audited: "students counselled in 2025", "offers received" – with a date range.

## 7. WhatsApp playbook (conversion engine)
- **Auto-greeting:** *Namaste! Thanks for contacting Reasons Education. Tell us your name and which country you're considering, and a counsellor will reply [during office hours: Sun–Fri 10–5].*
- **Quick replies:** book a session · documents checklist · IELTS/PTE batch · fee schedule · office location.
- **Response target:** [OWNER sets, e.g. 2 working hours] and publish it.
- **Labels:** New · Qualified · Session booked · Documents · Applied · Visa · Departed.

## 8. Analytics & tracking
- **Events:** `callback_submit`, `enquiry_step1`, `enquiry_submit`, `whatsapp_click`, `call_click`, `directions_click`, `quiz_complete`, `pdf_request`.
- **UTM scheme:** `utm_source=facebook|instagram|google|gbp|whatsapp`, `utm_medium=organic|paid|post`, `utm_campaign=uk-sept-2027|canada-funds-2026`.
- **Lead fields to store:** source, page, destination, intake, English status, budget band, contact preference – the admin panel already supports lead status and timeline.
- **Weekly dashboard:** leads by source, first-response time, session-booked rate, session → signed rate, cost per lead.
- **Consent:** cookie banner; Meta Pixel / GA4 only after consent; no sensitive data in events.

## 9. 90-day plan

| Days | Focus | Deliverables |
|---|---|---|
| **1–14** | **Trust & compliance** | Licence number live; Fees page; one phone set; remove unprovable claims; team photos & bios; GBP updated |
| **15–30** | **New core pages** | Home, Study abroad, Services, IELTS, About, FAQ, Contact, Canada, UK, USA, Australia (from `pages/`) |
| **31–45** | **SEO & speed** | Titles/meta, schema, internal links, sitemap dates, hero video fix, AVIF/WebP, GA4 events |
| **46–60** | **Content engine** | 4 blog posts (UK funds, NOC, F-1 changes, Germany blocked account); 2 PDFs; 8 short videos |
| **61–75** | **Proof** | 5 consented stories with documents; 20 Google reviews target (organic ask); office/team video |
| **76–90** | **Test & tune** | Small paid tests to country pages; A/B hero CTA wording; review GSC and fix weak pages |

## 10. KPIs (set after a 30-day baseline)
Impressions & clicks (GSC) · GBP calls/directions · site → WhatsApp/call rate · enquiry completion · session-booked rate · session → signed rate · average first-response time · review count/rating · IELTS/PTE enquiries per month.

## 11. Risk list
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Regulation details/dates change | Monthly check of MoEST notices; keep a "Compliance" owner |
| Rule change makes a page wrong | Review calendar; date every fact |
| Ads or posts imply guarantees | Pre-publish checklist (no guarantee words, licence no.) |
| Unlicensed image/video use | Replace unknown-licence photos with owned photography |
| Over-reliance on one channel | Keep GBP + Facebook + WhatsApp + referrals running together |
