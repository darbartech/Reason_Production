## A. Nepal – the rules that apply to Reasons itself

| Topic | What was found | Status | Confirm at |
|---|---|---|---|
| **New consultancy regulation** | Cabinet approved the *Educational Consultancy, Language Teaching and Preparation Classes (Operation and Management) Regulation, 2026 (2083 BS)* on **8 July 2026**. Effective date not stated in reports; industry groups protested in July. | ✅ (existence) / ⚠️ (current status) | Ministry of Education, Science & Technology (MoEST) notice / Nepal Gazette |
| **Licence** | One-year operating licence, renewed yearly; security deposit reported as **Rs 2.5 million** (head office). Test-prep / language classes need separate permission (deposit and fee figures differ between reports). | ✅ / ⚠️ details | MoEST / Gazette |
| **Website & fee disclosure** | Consultancies must **publish service charges on the official website** and in a citizen charter, and keep them updated. | ✅ | MoEST / Gazette |
| **Advertising limits** | No unrealistic guarantees of admission, scholarship, visa, jobs or PR; no false or exaggerated information; no undisclosed commissions or gifts from foreign institutions. | ✅ | MoEST / Gazette |
| **Liability** | A consultancy can be liable if bad advice leaves a student stranded or enrolled at an unauthorised institution. Recommended foreign institutions must be accredited and authorised to enrol international students. | ✅ | MoEST / Gazette |
| **Counsellor qualification** | Chief counsellor: bachelor's degree + recognised counselling training. | ✅ | MoEST / Gazette |
| **Grading** | Consultancies graded A/B/C by the Ministry. | ✅ | MoEST / Gazette |
| **Study-abroad fairs** | Need Ministry approval; fees apply. | ✅ | MoEST / Gazette |
| **NOC & bank payment** | Students must hold a **No Objection Certificate (NOC)** before paying tuition abroad through banks. Process details not confirmed. | ✅ (rule) / ⚠️ (process) | MoEST NOC portal |

**Content consequences:** show the licence number; publish a Fees page; delete every "high acceptance rate" / "top-rated" style claim; keep "we can't guarantee a visa or offer" (it is now a legal safe-harbour, not just good manners).
