## The short version

1. **Your instinct is right.** The site isn't short of facts; it repeats the same promises and steps in many places, and the facts that matter most (money, dates, fees, licence) are thin or missing.
2. **Three things changed in 2026 that the site doesn't reflect yet:**
   - **Nepal's new consultancy regulation (Cabinet, 8 Jul 2026):** licence disclosure, **published fees on your website**, no guarantees/exaggerated claims, NOC before tuition payment.
   - **Visa figures moved:** Canada **CAD 23,448** (1 Sep 2026) · UK **£1,570 / £1,203 from 30 Nov 2026** · Australia visa fee **AUD 2,000** · US **fixed stay period from 15 Sep 2026** · UK Graduate route cut to 18 months from 1 Jan 2027 and the **28 Oct Budget** promising 2 years for some universities.
   - **Search is answer-first:** pages that give a dated answer, a named reviewer and an official source get quoted.
3. **The rewrite removes noise and adds proof:** Home from ~1,500 → ~500 words; five "why us" lists → one; one process; one CTA; every figure dated and sourced.
4. **Some claims must go** (e.g., "top-rated", "high acceptance rate", "native accents", "over 10 years", "university relationships"). They are unprovable and risky under the new rules.
